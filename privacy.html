Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who this applies to

The Studio Delivery Tool is a private desktop tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Google data the tool accesses

With the permissions granted at Google sign-in, the tool:

  • reads the email address of the signed-in Google account, to confirm that the correct account is used;
  • reads the details of one configured Google Drive folder (name, whether files can be added, its sharing settings) without changing those settings;
  • uploads video files to that folder, reads the details of those files (name, size, checksum, link) to confirm the upload, and may replace a file it uploaded earlier with a newer version;
  • reads and writes cells in configured Google Sheets planning spreadsheets (video title, Drive link, status, caption, and in one workflow reads a comments column).

The tool does not delete files, does not change sharing settings, and does not access Gmail, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.

Where data is stored

  • The Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • A local log and a local status database (file names, checksums, Drive links, sheet row numbers, timestamps) are kept on the owner's computer.
  • There is no server. No data is sent anywhere other than to Google's own APIs.

Sharing and use

Google user data is used only to perform the delivery tasks described above. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local logs and status data stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that, the tool can no longer access Google data.
